About this prototype

Static design comps, not a running system — nothing here is wired to the venue. Every rate, reason code, hash, check name and identifier is taken from the implementation: its fixtures, a scenario run, and a real 43-check verification of a commitment bundle.

Screens are drawn at 1440×1040 (the phone at 390×844) and scale down to fit narrower windows.
